# Code of Conduct

DocFlow follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, but correct problems clearly.
- Keep discussions useful for improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants whose behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive.
