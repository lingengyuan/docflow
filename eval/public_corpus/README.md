# Public Domain Retrieval Corpus

This small corpus is a public, reproducible smoke benchmark for DocFlow retrieval.
It is not a broad external benchmark such as BEIR, MTEB, or C-MTEB.

Sources:

- United States Constitution, Article I. Public domain United States government text.
- First Amendment to the United States Constitution. Public domain United States government text.
- Abraham Lincoln, Gettysburg Address. Public domain text.
- NASA Apollo 11 public historical summary. United States government material.
- Lewis Carroll, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland. Public domain text in the United States.
- Jane Austen, Pride and Prejudice. Public domain text in the United States.

The cases in `eval/public_retrieval_v1.jsonl` point at these files and should be
run without source filtering.
