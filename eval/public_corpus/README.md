# Public Domain Retrieval Corpus

This small corpus is a public, reproducible smoke benchmark for DocFlow retrieval.
It is not a broad external benchmark such as BEIR, MTEB, or C-MTEB.

Sources:

- United States Constitution, Preamble and Article I. Public domain United States government text.
- First, Second, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, and Eighth Amendments to the United States Constitution. Public domain United States government text.
- United States Declaration of Independence. Public domain text.
- Abraham Lincoln, Gettysburg Address. Public domain text.
- Abraham Lincoln, Second Inaugural Address. Public domain text.
- George Washington, Farewell Address. Public domain text.
- Federalist No. 10. Public domain text.
- NASA Apollo 11, Moon, Mars rover, Hubble, and Webb public facts. United States government material.
- Lewis Carroll, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland. Public domain text in the United States.
- Jane Austen, Pride and Prejudice. Public domain text in the United States.
- Jane Austen, Emma. Public domain text in the United States.
- Charlotte Bronte, Jane Eyre. Public domain text in the United States.
- Mary Shelley, Frankenstein. Public domain text in the United States.
- Herman Melville, Moby-Dick. Public domain text in the United States.
- Bram Stoker, Dracula. Public domain text in the United States.
- Arthur Conan Doyle, A Scandal in Bohemia. Public domain text in the United States.
- Charles Dickens, A Tale of Two Cities. Public domain text in the United States.
- L. Frank Baum, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz. Public domain text in the United States.

The cases in `eval/public_retrieval_v1.jsonl` point at these files and should be
run without source filtering.
